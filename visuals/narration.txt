You have sixteen repair crews across eight sites. Where should they go for the
next seventy-two hours?

Equal staffing sounds reasonable. But incident demand varies, and some sites
accumulate queues while others have spare capacity.

Here, both policies receive exactly the same incidents. SimPy models the queues.
A mixed-integer optimizer chooses where to assign the available crews.

The animation is one held-out scenario. To evaluate the method, we also run an
independent, sixty-four-site benchmark across sixteen replications. The result
and uncertainty interval are calculated directly from those runs.

Then we double the incident arrival intensity. The allocation stays fixed, and
the remaining backlog shows its capacity limits.

This is an independent synthetic study. The code, assumptions, tests, and
reproducible evidence are public.
